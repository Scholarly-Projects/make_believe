1906 Miller-Dervant Costume Catalog

Early Spokane Imitative Identities

This visualization highlights the categories of costumes from the leading company in Spokane, Washington from their 1906 catalog.

The wall has two faces. The front organizes costumes by type: military, theatrical, royal and supernatural. The reverse organizes the same catalog organized by the ethnic identity being imitated. Entries are sorted in order of frequency; select any tile to read more.

Drag around the wall's border, or use the Flip button, to turn it over.

Source: Miller-Dervant Catalog, 1906, Miller-Dervant Company Records, 1896–1955, MsSC 198, Eastern Washington State Historical Society, Northwest Museum of Arts and Culture, Spokane, WA.

Controls
Tab Move between tiles, from most to least frequent
Enter Open selected tile's detail view
Esc Close detail view or this introduction
F Flip the wall to the other face
drag Click and drag the border ring to rotate
