1906 Miller-Dervant Catalog Visualization

Imitative Identities

This visualization highlights the materials of the leading costume company in Spokane, Washington from their 1906 catalog.

The wall has two faces. The front organizes costumes by type: military, theatrical, royal, etc. The reverse organizes the same catalog by ethnic identity. Entries are sorted in order of frequency. Select any tile to read more.

Select Costume Type or Ethnic Identity at the top of the wall, or press F to flip between views.

Source: Miller-Dervant Catalog, 1906, Miller-Dervant Company Records, 1896–1955, MsSC 198, Eastern Washington State Historical Society, Northwest Museum of Arts and Culture, Spokane, WA.

Controls
Tab Move between tiles, from most to least frequent
Enter Open selected tile's detail view
Esc Close detail view or this introduction
F Flip the wall to the other face
